# DungeonPrep

**DungeonPrep** is a World of Warcraft Classic addon designed to bring modern convenience to classic dungeon running. It serves as a seamlessly integrated, natively-styled dungeon companion that helps you track quests, manage party syncs, and verify key attunements before you step foot in the instance.

![DungeonPrep Showcase](https://via.placeholder.com/800x400.png?text=DungeonPrep+UI)

## Features

* **Interactive Quest Tracker**: Automatically scans your quest log against all available quests for your selected dungeon. Displays exactly what you have, what you're missing, and what you've already completed.
* **Smart Nameplate Objectives**: Injects classic ! icons onto the nameplates of mobs that drop quest items or are objectives for your dungeon quests.
* **Loot Reminders**: Fires a massive raid warning when a quest objective boss dies, reminding you to loot the essential items before the party moves on.
* **Party Sync Matrix**: Instantly cross-reference your dungeon quests with your party members to see who is missing which quest, ensuring the whole group is aligned.
* **Keys & Locks Tracker**: A detailed breakdown of every dungeon key and attunement in the game, complete with sources and unlock descriptions. Automatically checks your bags and keyring to track your progress.
* **Quest Sharing**: One-click broadcast of your current dungeon quests to party chat.
* **Native WoW Aesthetic**: Designed carefully using standard World of Warcraft UI textures and templates to feel like an authentic extension of the classic client.

## Installation

1. Download the latest release from [CurseForge](#) or the [Releases page](#).
2. Extract the DungeonPrep folder.
3. Place it in your World of Warcraft AddOns directory:
   _classic_era_\Interface\AddOns\DungeonPrep
4. Log into the game and ensure the addon is enabled.

## Usage

* Click the **DungeonPrep minimap button** to toggle the main UI.
* Use the **Left Sidebar** to filter by bracket (Beta/All) and select your target dungeon.
* Navigate through the **Quests**, **Party Sync**, and **Keys & Locks** tabs to prepare for your run!
